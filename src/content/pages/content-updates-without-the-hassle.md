{% section "hero", "Content Updates Without the Hassle" %}
Websites are not static.

Prices change. Staff move on. Services evolve. New projects are completed. Blog posts need publishing. Landing pages need refining.

In theory, content management systems make this easy. In practice, most business owners don't have the time, patience, or desire to log in and make changes themselves.

That's where I come in.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "You don't need to log in" %}
If you'd rather not touch the backend of your website, you don't have to.

You can simply send through what you need by email or message:

- Updated text
- New images
- A new team member bio
- A new service description
- A blog post draft
- Event details
- Pricing changes    

I'll take care of the formatting, placement, and publishing so it looks consistent and professional.

No passwords. No dashboards. No risk of accidentally breaking something.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Small changes done properly" %}
What looks like a small change on the surface can sometimes affect layout, spacing, image sizes, or SEO structure behind the scenes.

When I make updates, I:

- Ensure formatting matches the rest of your site
- Optimise images before uploading
- Maintain consistent headings and structure
- Check that links work correctly
- Confirm the page displays properly on mobile

You get a finished result that fits naturally into your existing site.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Adding new pages and sections" %}
As your business grows, your website should reflect that.

I can help with:

- Creating new service pages
- Adding landing pages for campaigns
- Publishing blog posts
- Expanding FAQs
- Updating case studies or project portfolios    

If you're unsure how something should be structured, I can guide that too. Sometimes the question isn't just "can you upload this?" but "how should this be presented?"
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Ongoing refinement" %}
Websites benefit from small, regular improvements.

That might mean:

- Tweaking layout for clarity
- Adjusting calls to action
- Cleaning up outdated content
- Improving readability
- Simplifying navigation    

You don't always need a redesign. Often, thoughtful incremental updates keep a site feeling current and effective.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Support that fits around your schedule" %}
You're busy running a business.

Instead of blocking out time to wrestle with a CMS, you can send through what you need changed and get back to more important work.

You won't be left wondering whether it's been done properly. You won't need to second-guess formatting decisions. You won't accidentally publish half-finished drafts.

It's handled.
{% endsection %}

Outdated content quietly erodes trust.

A website that reflects your current services, team, and direction builds confidence. Regular updates show that your business is active and attentive.

If keeping your content up to date feels like another task on an already long list, I can take that off your hands.
