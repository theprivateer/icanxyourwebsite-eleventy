{% section "section", "When your website becomes a problem" %}
Many websites start out fine and slowly degrade over time. Common issues I help clients with include:

- Slow websites that frustrate visitors and hurt conversions
- Poor or under-performing SEO that makes your site hard to find
- Broken WordPress sites, updates, or plugins
- Websites that are difficult or risky to update
- Designs that do not work properly on mobile devices
- Layouts that break across browsers or screen sizes
- Sites that no longer meet accessibility expectations

If your site feels fragile or you are afraid to touch it in case something breaks, that is a strong sign it needs attention.

[Read more](/when-your-website-becomes-a-problem)
{% endsection %}
