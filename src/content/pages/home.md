{% section "section", "Clean design, strong SEO, and solid performance" %}
A good website should load quickly, be easy to use, and make sense to both people and search engines. I focus on clean, uncluttered design paired with well-structured code that performs well across devices and connection speeds.

Search engine optimisation is built in from the start, not bolted on later. That means clear page structure, meaningful content, fast load times, and technical foundations that help search engines understand your site.

Performance is not just a technical detail. Faster websites keep visitors engaged, reduce drop-offs, and tend to rank better in search results. Every decision, from layout to code, is made with performance in mind.
