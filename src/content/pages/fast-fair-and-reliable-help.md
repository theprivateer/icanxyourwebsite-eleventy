{% section "hero", "Fair, reliable, and honest about timing" %}
When your website has a problem, you do not want a sales pitch.

You want it fixed.

My approach is simple: understand the issue properly, explain what is going on in plain language, and solve it without unnecessary drama.

No inflated proposals. No pressure to rebuild everything. No long contracts designed to lock you in. And no promises about speed that I cannot keep.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Priced fairly, scoped realistically" %}
Website problems can feel urgent, which makes it easy to overspend out of stress.

I price work competitively and fairly, based on what actually needs to be done. If a focused fix will solve the issue, that is what I will recommend. If something larger is required, I will explain why and outline the options clearly.

You will always understand:

- What the problem is    
- What it will take to fix
- What it will cost
- What the longer-term implications are

Clarity builds trust. Surprises do not.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "When I will actually get to it" %}
Plenty of websites in this market promise rapid emergency response. Most of the people making that promise cannot keep it, and I would rather tell you what is true.

I have a day job and I am one person. I am not a 24-hour helpdesk, and anyone offering you one at this price is guessing.

What I will do:

- Answer a new enquiry within two to three business days
- Tell you honestly when I can start, before you commit to anything
- Actually start when I said I would

If you are on an ongoing care plan, you get more than that. I will acknowledge you within one business day and start work within two. If your site is down, I will acknowledge it the same day and start that evening.

No 24/7 anything. Ever.

That is less impressive than "we drop everything". Unlike "we drop everything", it is true, and you can hold me to it.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "No unnecessary rebuilds" %}
It is common for business owners to be told, "You need a new website."

Sometimes that is true. Often it is not.

In many cases, the site can be stabilised, cleaned up, and improved without starting from scratch. A rebuild should only happen when it is truly the most sensible long-term option, not because it is the most profitable one.

If repair makes sense, we repair.

If refinement makes sense, we refine.

If rebuilding is the best path, I will say so and explain why.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Direct access, not a support queue" %}
When you work with me, you deal directly with the person diagnosing and fixing your site.

There is no ticket escalation.

No account manager relaying messages.

No junior developer guessing at the problem.

That means:

- Faster understanding of the issue    
- Clearer communication
- Fewer misunderstandings
- More accountability

You can ask questions and get straight answers.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Clear communication throughout" %}
Technical problems can feel intimidating if you are not technical yourself.

Part of my job is translating complexity into something understandable. I will explain what happened, what was done, and how to avoid similar issues in future, without jargon.

You should feel informed, not overwhelmed.
{% endsection %}

{% section "section", "Dependable results" %}
Fixing the immediate issue is only part of the job. The goal is to leave your website more stable than it was before.

That may include:

- Improving update processes    
- Putting proper backups in place
- Removing fragile components
- Strengthening security
- Simplifying how the site is managed

The aim is not just to get you back online, but to reduce the chance you need emergency help again anytime soon.
{% endsection %}

Fair in how work is scoped and priced.

Reliable in both communication and results.
